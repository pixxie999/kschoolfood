Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

K-School Food ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website kschoolfood.com. We collect minimal information to provide and improve our service:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, referral source, browser type, and device information via Google Analytics.
  • User-Submitted Reviews: Star ratings and optional comments you voluntarily post on recipe pages. We store your approximate country (derived from IP) but do not store your IP address.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense). You can control cookies through your browser settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display and improve our recipe content
  • To analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • To moderate user-submitted reviews

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: Website traffic analysis. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense: Advertising. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can opt out at Google Ads Settings.
  • Affiliate Programs: We participate in affiliate programs (Amazon Associates, iHerb). Clicking affiliate links may set cookies on those platforms.

4. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained per Google's default policies. User reviews are retained indefinitely unless deletion is requested.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to or deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Disable cookies in your browser settings

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at pixxie999@gmail.com.
