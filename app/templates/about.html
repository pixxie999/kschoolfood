About K-School Food

K-School Food brings Korean school lunch culture to the world. We translate real daily menus from Korean schools into easy-to-follow English recipes so anyone can cook authentic Korean dishes at home.

Our Mission

Korean school lunches are famous worldwide for their variety, nutrition, and delicious taste. But finding actual recipes in English has always been a challenge. K-School Food bridges that gap by:

  • Fetching real daily menus from the Korean NEIS (National Education Information System) public API
  • Translating each dish into clear English recipes with step-by-step instructions
  • Suggesting locally available ingredient substitutions for international cooks
  • Presenting menus in a fun 5-compartment meal tray format — just like Korean school cafeterias

How It Works

📡

1. Collect

We pull daily school lunch menus from Korea's official NEIS open data system

✍️

2. Translate

Each dish is translated with proper English names, ingredients, and cooking steps

🍱

3. Serve

Browse today's menu, explore recipes, and cook Korean school food at home

Data Sources

All school lunch data comes from the NEIS Open API, a public government data service operated by the Korean Ministry of Education. This data is freely available and regularly updated.
